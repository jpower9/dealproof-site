Common questions

How are the numbers calculated? Every formula is listed in the app: open any deal and tap How these are calculated (also in Settings → Formulas).

Why are there two DSCRs? Investors look at NOI ÷ debt service. DSCR lenders qualify on gross rent ÷ PITIA (principal, interest, taxes, insurance, HOA). DealProof shows both.

Where do the loan rules come from? Fannie Mae's eligibility matrix (investment and owner-occupied down payments, PMI), HUD Handbook 4000.1 and Mortgagee Letters (FHA upfront and annual MIP, the 3–4 unit self-sufficiency test). Lenders add their own overlays — confirm with yours.

How do I restore my purchase? Settings → Restore purchases (or on the upgrade screen). Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I move my deals to a new phone? Settings → Export backup, then Import backup on the new phone. DealProof has no account or server, so this file is your copy.

Cancel a subscription: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → DealProof.
