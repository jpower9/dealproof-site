DealProof Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

DealProof analyzes rental-property deals from the numbers you enter. It is built so that your deals never leave your iPhone. We don't collect any data.

What stays on your device

  • Deals you enter — prices, rents, expenses, loan terms, notes and addresses.
  • Photos you choose — an optional property photo and an optional logo for your Deal Report. The app only receives the single image you pick; it can't see the rest of your photo library.
  • Settings — default assumptions, your deal criteria and the optional name, company, phone and email printed on your Deal Report.

This data is stored only in the app's storage on your iPhone. Backups and PDF reports exist only if you export or share them yourself.

What we do NOT do

  • No account, sign-in, server or cloud database.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. No third-party SDKs at all.
  • We never sell or share your data — we never receive it.

Apple services the app uses

  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We never see your payment details.

Deleting your data

Delete deals in the app, or delete the app to remove everything. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

DealProof is not directed to children under 13.

Not advice

DealProof's results are estimates for analysis only — not investment, tax, legal or lending advice. Verify all figures and loan terms with licensed professionals.
